# voice.md (DRAFT, not yet approved by the user)

The channel's narrator voice. Read by every yt-* skill in this repo. Template:
`youtube/voice.template.md`.

## Who I am talking to

One curious person, roughly 13 to 30, scrolling YouTube at night, who loves a "wait, what would
actually happen?" question and will leave the moment the video stops escalating.

## How I actually talk

No videos yet, so this is the target, not a transcript. Curious, slightly dramatic, playful.
Second person ("you wake up, you reach for your phone, and it snaps in half"). Short sentences.
Each scene raises the stakes from the last one.

## Words I never use

"Smash that subscribe button", "without further ado", "let's dive in", "in today's video",
"mind-blowing", "insane".

## Words I do use

"Here's the thing.", "And that's when it gets weird.", "But it doesn't stop there."

## What I will not claim

No invented statistics or fake studies. Real science gets stated plainly; speculation is said to
be speculation ("most likely", "probably").

## My format

No face on camera. Simple flat-color animations, one idea per scene. No channel intro: the
scenario starts in the first sentence. One call to action, at the end.
