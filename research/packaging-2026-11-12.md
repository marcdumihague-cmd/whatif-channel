## Thumbnails of the top 6 (looked at, not stored)

- Text: 1-3 words, huge, white with a dark outline and one yellow word ("MY EX.", "MATCH", "MOM?!",
  "SEE THE 4TH DIMENSION?").
- A red arrow from the text to the subject (2 of 6).
- The power drawn as a glowing label over a person: a red timer "2 DAYS", name tags, heights,
  "FUTURE WIFE 99%" (4 of 6).
- One large emotional face: crying, smirking, blushing.
- Bright, saturated, characters fill the frame.

## Title/thumbnail changes that doubled views (what-if animation, last 3 months)

- "What If Humans Had a Tail?" -> "What If Humans NEVER Lost Their Tails?" (title + thumbnail): 67x views per hour.
- Thumbnail-only wins (12.8x, 16.7x, 2.3x): from showing the power to showing its consequence in 2-3
  words ("CAN'T HIDE IT", "CAN'T HIT ME." with a "DODGE: 100%" status tag).
- Caveat from vidIQ: picked because views rose after the change, so it shows correlation, not cause.
