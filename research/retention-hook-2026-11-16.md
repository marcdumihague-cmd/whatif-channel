# Payoff drop-off and the 2-second hook (2026-11-16)

Why: the user sees viewers leaving near the payoff in the channel's analytics, and wants the hook to land
within 2 seconds. Sources: a web search of 2025-26 retention guides (creator blogs and tool vendors: none is
a controlled study, so these are working rules to test against our own retention graphs), plus our own
transcript study (`transcript-study-2026-11.md`).

## Why viewers leave near the payoff

- They feel they already got what they came for, or the next beat doesn't look worth the wait.
- The run-up is slow: recaps, explaining, "and you remember the rule" lines right before the climax.
  (Episode 8 did this: 30 s of walking on stage and re-explaining before the trick.)
- The story signals it is wrapping up (a calm "So...", a summary, a wide establishing shot of the last place).
- All the release is saved for the end, with no smaller payoffs on the way.

## Rules from episode 9 on

1. **A second open loop runs through the payoff and closes in the last 20 seconds** (a twist that changes
   how the whole video looks). The main payoff must not be the last unanswered question.
2. **No recap and no explaining in the last third.** Anything the climax needs is planted earlier.
3. **The climax goes wrong first**: the plan the viewer expects fails, so the payoff can't be predicted.
4. **Speed up toward the end**: shorter lines and faster cuts in the last third, never slower.
5. **Small payoffs every 30-45 s** (a win, a number jumping, a joke landing), not one big one.
6. **The payoff is shown, not told**, and takes seconds.
7. **Nothing after the last reveal except the question** (under 6 s). No "so", no moral.

## The 2-second hook

- Frame 1 is the most striking image of the cold open, already moving, with 2-4 huge words on screen
  (many viewers start muted).
- The first sentence is under ~2 seconds and is the stake itself, not the place or time.
  ("Your crush just said no." not "Gym class, 10:14 a.m.")
- Second sentence = the contradiction that makes them need the next line ("And it doesn't matter.").
- Premise clear by ~0:20, not 0:45.
