# Research

Everything gathered for the channel. Conclusions live in `youtube/viral-notes.md` (writing),
`youtube/motion.md` (animation) and `CLAUDE.md` (pipeline decisions); this folder is the evidence.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `2026-11-niche/` | First niche scan: 298 videos from 16 "what if" channels, outlier ranking, premise types, the competitor searches for our premise, transcript picks |
| `2026-11-niche-v2/` | Bigger test: 2,505 videos from 90 channels, the 11 raw searches, and the within-channel permutation tests of the rules |

## Public repo: what is not stored here

The repo went public again on 2026-11-12. The 12 study transcripts, the user's pasted Otherwise
transcript and the 4 reference screenshots were removed from the files and from git history, because
they are other creators' copyrighted work. Only our own analysis of them stays
(`transcript-study-2026-11.md`, `reference-otherwise-reborn.md`). To study them again, re-fetch
transcripts with vidIQ (5 credits each) and keep them out of git.

## Not stored here, on purpose

- **Audio and video files** (voice samples, renders): too large and regenerable from the repo.
- YouTube still blocks transcript downloads from the cloud machine ("confirm you're not a bot").
  Getting around that check is off limits; use vidIQ or `scripts/get_transcripts.py` on the user's
  own computer.
