# Fact checks: "What If You Were the Last Liar on Earth?"

Checked 2026-11-11 before voicing.

| Claim in the script | Verdict | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| People spot lies about 54% of the time | ✓ (meta-analysis of 206 documents, 24,483 judges; weighted ~53.5%, "just under 54%") | Bond & DePaulo (2006), *Personality and Social Psychology Review* 10(3) — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16859438/) |
| The average person tells one or two lies a day | ✓ (mean 1.65/day among 1,000 US adults; heavily skewed, ~60% told none) | Serota, Levine & Boster (2010), *Human Communication Research* — [PDF](https://oakland.edu/Assets/upload/docs/News/2014/Serota-et-al-Prevalence-of-Lying-2010.pdf) |
| Counting cards isn't illegal in Las Vegas, but casinos can ban you | ✓ (mental counting is not a crime under Nevada law; casinos may refuse service; devices are a felony) | [Las Vegas Advisor](https://www.lasvegasadvisor.com/gambling-with-an-edge/blackjack/card-counting-not-illegal), [Shouse Law](https://www.shouselaw.com/nv/blog/is-card-counting-a-crime-in-las-vegas) |
| 7-2 is the worst starting hand in hold'em | ✓ (standard poker knowledge) | — |
| The first number on the table drags the final price toward it | ✓ (standard result, not re-checked online) | Galinsky & Mussweiler (2001), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* |
| SVB customers tried to pull out $42 billion in one day; regulators shut it the next morning | ✓ ($42B requested March 9, 2023, ~25% of deposits; DFPI took possession March 10) | [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/03/11/silicon-valley-bank-run-42-billion-attempted-withdrawals-in-one-day/), [California DFPI order](https://dfpi.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/337/2023/03/DFPI-Orders-Silicon-Valley-Bank-03102023.pdf) |
| Many everyday lies are told to protect someone else | ✓ (kept without a number on purpose) | DePaulo et al. (1996), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* |
