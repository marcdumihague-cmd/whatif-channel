# When to upload (2026-11-13)

Web search of 2026 timing studies (marketing blogs; methods mostly not published, so treat as a starting point):
- Weekdays 2-5 PM ET, strongest Wed-Fri (PostPlanify, SocialPilot).
- Sunday is strong for long videos: Buffer (1.8M videos) says Sunday morning; Descript says Sunday 2-4 PM ET.
- 6,175-post study (postfa.st): 5 PM UTC (about noon ET) best.
- Shorts peak at different times than long videos (SocialPilot).
- Common rule: publish 2-3 hours before your own audience's peak.

Our plan until the channel has data: long videos Thursday or Sunday, published about 12-2 PM ET; space
uploads a few days apart; then switch to YouTube Studio > Analytics > Audience > "When your viewers are
on YouTube" once it appears (it needs enough viewers). Timing matters far less than title + thumbnail.
