## What it shows

1. **The template is table stakes, not the difference.** The best flops (Odds, Predict the Market,
   Suddenly You's Lie) have almost every structural element of the hits: a vivid open, a flash-forward
   promise, "that's real" facts, a cost section, a timed choice climax, a sequel tease. Same channel,
   same template: Crush 25x vs Odds 0.1x; Reborn 15.6x vs Predict the Market 0.1x.
2. **What separates them is the premise's pull on a person.** Hits are about a crush, a dad, a mom,
   humanity, your own body. The flops are about odds, trading, and a world of rude strangers. Money as
   the premise flopped even with the hit's characters (Rocky, the bus-driver dad) copied over.
3. **Saturated premises flop.** "Everyone disappeared except you" already exists at 4.2M views; the
   Ifverse version did 0.13x. Our premise checks matter.
4. **Length.** Hits are 7-10 min (Stick Saga's 17 min is the exception); two flops run 13-19 min.
   Suddenly You's Lie has What If Hero's structure at 2.3x the length and did 0.1x.
5. **Keep "you" unnamed.** The only flop that names the hero ("This is you. Gabe") also states its
   premise last (1:15). Weak evidence, one video.
6. **The climax is a selfless choice under a timer** in 5 of 7 hits: the power is used for someone else
   at a cost to you (the World Cup, the girl you like, your secret, your money).
7. **Openings vary**: a demonstration (3 hits), in-medias-res (2), an emotional moment (1), an event (1).
   What they share: something happens in the first 5 seconds and the premise is clear by ~0:45.
8. **Plant and pay off** appears in 4 of 7 hits (Riley's thread, line three, the late laugh, Zoe's score)
   and only 1 flop.

## Caveats

12 videos; channel size, thumbnails and upload timing also differ. Structure can't be ruled out, only
shown not to be sufficient. Titles/thumbnails weren't compared here (vidIQ can fetch thumbnails).
